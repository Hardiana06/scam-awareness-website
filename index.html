Homepage

Scams are fraudulent schemes designed to deceive people and their personal information.
Common types of scams include phishing emails, fake online stores, and lottery frauds.
To identify a scam, look for warning signs such as unsolicited messages, requests for personal
information or payment, and poor grammar or spelling. Verify the legitimacy of the source by
checking official websites or contacting the company directly, and always be cautious when
sharing personal details online. When serving the Web, Web users should be vigilent and look out
for 'Too good to be true' deals as it will probably be a SCAM.
































3 Simple Ways To Prevent Falling For Scams

Follow this ACT

  1. Add security features and set up transaction limits for internet banking transactions, enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA), Multifactor Authentication for bank accounts. Consider Money Lock option for your bank accounts.
  2. Check for any spelling errors in the webpage link for tell-tale signs of a phishing website. Call the NCPC Anti-Scam Helpline at 1800-722-6688 if you are unsure if a situation you are facing is a scam. Be cautious of attractive deals that seem too good to be true.
  3. Tell the authorities by filing a police report and contact your bank immediately if you suspect that your account or credit card has been compromised.
