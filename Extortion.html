Extortion scams

What is Extortion Scam?

It is when scammers threaten you to get money from you, posing as representatives of a company and may threaten you and demand payment.

How do scammers use extortion to scam?

Scammers frequently use cyber-extortion to coerce you into complying with their demands; this tactic typically involves attempts to obtain your banking information or threats of violence if you don't give them large sums of money.

How can we avoid getting scammed?

To prevent unauthorised access to devices and accounts,
Follow the following steps
-Change your password regularly; use a long and random password/password that contains a mix of upper and lower case letters, numbers and symbols
-Do not use personally identifiable information (eg your name, date of birth, NRIC) in your passwords
-Do not use the same password for multiple accounts
-Enable two-factor authentication when available
-Run a virus scan on all your devices
-Keep your computers and mobile devices up-to-date and protected with up-to-date antivirus software.
